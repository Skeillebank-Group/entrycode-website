How it works

Four steps, from request to scan.

1.

Apply or book

Attendees request access or buy a ticket directly through your venue's page.

2.

Approve

Your venue decides who's in. Review each request yourself, or let members, or everyone, through automatically. Buying a ticket alone doesn't get anyone in.

3.

Scan at the door

A signed, rotating code is checked in seconds — nothing to forge or pass along. Guests have already paid, so there's no cash at the door.

4.

Stay open, online or off

If the connection drops, entry keeps running and reconciles automatically once it's back.

Built for doors that can't afford to fail.

This is where EntryCode does the most work — and where most ticketing platforms stop trying.

Signed, rotating codes

no static image to copy

Every entry code is cryptographically signed and changes over time. A screenshot from last week is worthless at tonight's door.

Works offline

local fallback + auto reconcile

If the venue's connection drops, scanning falls back to a locally cached code and syncs automatically the moment it's back online — no gap in coverage at the door.

Your members, your data

fully isolated per venue

Every venue's member list, applicants, and attendee history stay completely separate — never visible to, or shared with, any other venue on EntryCode.

You decide who's in

venue makes the call

EntryCode handles the mechanics — identity, access, payment plumbing. Every accept, reject, and vetting decision stays with your venue.
