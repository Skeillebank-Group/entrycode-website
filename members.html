Private doors, handled privately.

For members' clubs and adult venues, who gets in matters, and so does who knows about it. Here's how EntryCode handles both.

Nobody gets in just by paying.

On EntryCode, a ticket is a request, not a purchase. Your venue decides who gets approved.

You decide who's approved

Review each request yourself, or approve members, or everyone, automatically. You can change that for a single event. Requests you don't answer in time expire and count as declined.

Nothing is charged until you say yes

Guests only pay after approval, by Open Banking straight from their bank to yours. Declined guests are never charged.

Bans and revoked tickets

Ban a guest from future events, or revoke an approved ticket before they've checked in. The decision stays with you.

Recognise your members without handing over your list.

Guests say whether they're a member or want to apply. That's their claim. You make the final call, with help from your own records.

1.

Upload a hashed list

Match returning members from a spreadsheet. EntryCode stores only keyed hashes of contact details, never the plain emails or phone numbers.

2.

Or connect your own system

If you already run a membership database, EntryCode can check requests against it through your own API.

3.

Members-only events

Run events for members only, or give members priority, and keep applicants separate until you've decided.

4.

Your list stays yours

Each venue's members, applicants and history are kept apart and never shown to, or shared with, another venue.

Built with adult venues in mind.

A visit to an adult venue can reveal something personal about a guest. The law treats that as especially sensitive data, and so does EntryCode.

Explicit consent first

separate, never pre-ticked

Before a guest's first request to any adult venue, EntryCode asks for their explicit consent and records when they gave it. They can withdraw it at any time.

Discreet notifications

no venue or event names

Push notifications and emails about adult venues never name the venue or the event. The details are only visible inside EntryCode.

18+ only

you still check ID

EntryCode is for people aged 18 and over and asks for a date of birth once. That's a self-declared check, not proof of age. You still check ID at the door, and you can set a higher minimum age for your events.

Tickets that don't linger

PIN-locked, auto-removed

Tickets saved on a guest's phone are protected by a PIN, wiped after five wrong tries, and removed a few days after the event.

Who's responsible for what.

You decide who gets into your events, so you're the data controller for your guest list. EntryCode handles that data on your behalf under a data processing agreement, stored in the EU. Your licences, age policy and house rules remain yours.
